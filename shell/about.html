Why these are free

Every tool here replaces a product that lets you do most of the work for free and then charges at the moment you need the result: the download button, the export, the minute limit, the watermark. That is not a price for value delivered. It is a toll placed where you are least able to walk away.

These tools do the thing you came for, at full quality, with no account. They can be free because they run entirely in your browser. There is no server processing your data, so there is nothing to pay for per user, and nothing you type is ever uploaded.

How it stays honest

  • The core action is never behind a paywall. If a paid option ever appears it will be for convenience only, and the free version will keep working.
  • Nothing leaves your device. Autosave uses your browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time.
  • The code is open source under MIT. Read it, fork it, host it yourself.
