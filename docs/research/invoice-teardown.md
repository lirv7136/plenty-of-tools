Earlier notes, checked: "Invoice Ninja free allows 5 clients" is **confirmed**. "Xero Ignite caps
at 20 invoices a month" is **only partly confirmed**: the Ignite page says "For more than 20
invoices, see the Grow plan" and does not say "a month", and the main AU pricing page no longer
lists Ignite. Do not quote it. "Invoice Home free stops at $1,000 per 30 days" is **confirmed**.

**What the table says.** The generous free tiers (Zoho AU, Square AU, Wave, PayPal) already give
away recurring invoices and reminders, but every one needs an account, and Square and PayPal earn
on the payment. The no account generators (ScaleSuite, Rounded's free tool) save nothing.
Nobody studied offers a date on each line without an account or a paid plan.

## 2. What users praise and what they complain about

Sources: Apple App Store review pages (AU and US), ProductReview.com.au, and Trustpilot to fill
gaps. **Reddit was blocked to the research tools; nothing here comes from Reddit.** Google Play
pages returned no review text. App Store reviews have no permalinks, so the link is to the
review page and the review title is quoted.

**Praised most**
1. **Fast and easy, often on a phone.** Many times. Invoice Home "Seamless Invoicing" ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/invoice-maker-by-invoice-home/id1435778692?see-all=reviews)); Invoice Ninja "As easy as pie" ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/invoice-ninja/id1503970375?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)); Rounded, simpler than Xero ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/rounded-invoicing-accounting/id1071070951?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).
2. **Free, or no subscription.** Many times. Square "So far so good" ([AU reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/square-invoices-invoice-maker/id1405254630?see-all=reviews)); Zoho "Fantastic and free", moved from Invoice2go over price rises ([AU reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/zoho-invoice-maker-app/id413017364?see-all=reviews)).
3. **Invoices that look professional.** Many times. Square, a sole trader locksmith; Invoice Home "Quick and Easy"; the Lessons app, parents find invoices clear ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lessons-invoicing-email-pdf/id717994393?see-all=reviews)).
4. **Automation: recurring, reminders, paid or unpaid at a glance.** Several times. Zoho "Love it!"; Square "Completely changes the way I operate"; Invoice Ninja, invoices from timesheets.
5. **Support, and Australian GST and BAS handled.** Several times. Rounded (four reviews); Xero on [ProductReview](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/xero).

**Complaints that recur**
1. **Price rises and invoice caps.** The strongest signal. Invoice Simple ([AU reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/invoice-simple-invoice-maker/id694831622?see-all=reviews)): "Price goes up, quality stays the same", "Very dodgy business", "App is now too expensive". Invoice2go ([AU reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/invoice-2go-easy-quote-maker/id540236748?see-all=reviews)): "From Great to really bad"; 2026 reviews on [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/invoice.2go.com) report rises of 50% or more. Xero ([AU reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/xero-accounting-for-business/id441880705?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)): "July 2024 plan changes", "Predatory pricing".
2. **Money held or accounts frozen by the payment side.** Many times. Wave ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wave-small-business-software/id881629660?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)); Square ([ProductReview](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/squareup)); PayPal "Bad for business" ([AU reviews](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/paypal-business/id1053148887?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).
3. **Updates that add clicks, and bugs.** Many times. Xero "Please bring back approve and add payment"; Invoice2go "Unreliable" (invoices vanished).
4. **Free that turns into a forced subscription.** Several times. Invoice Simple "Not that happy" (subscription after two invoices); Invoice2go "DO NOT BUY" (lifetime licence replaced).
5. **Hard to cancel, weak support.** Several times. Xero "Poor experience"; Square on ProductReview; Invoice2go on Trustpilot.

Tested and not found: complaints about needing an account to make one invoice. Weak (two cases):
poor GST support. Medium: too complex for one person (mostly Xero); losing invoices on leaving
(Invoice2go).

**People paid by the visit (low confidence, thin evidence).** Tutors log each lesson and invoice
weekly or monthly (Lessons app). NDIS apps such as [EasyAs](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/easyas-provider-invoicing/id6740611906) and [Earni](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/earni/id6738632246) load support item codes and prices; they have very few reviews. Mobile workers are often paid on the spot and want a one step "paid now". A reviewer on [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/invoicesimple.com) who sends many small invoices says caps hurt most. Reddit needs a manual read before this section is trusted.

## 4. NDIS invoices: can the rules be met exactly?

**Caveat:** ndis.gov.au refused every direct fetch (403), so the rules below were read from search
excerpts of the official pages. **Read these pages by hand before building anything.**

- **Fields** ([Invoicing and record keeping](https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/working-provider/getting-paid/invoicing-and-record-keeping)): provider name and ABN; participant name and NDIS number; support item number; date or dates of each support; quantity or hours and unit price; claim type (for example provider travel, short notice cancellation); total and any GST. One participant per invoice, many supports allowed. Plan managers need the provider's ABN.
- **Price limits** ([PAPL 2025-26](https://ndis.gov.au/media/7739/download), [Pricing Schedule 2026-27](https://www.ndis.gov.au/media/8703/download), effective 24 Sep 2026): plan managers must not pay above the limits, whether the provider is registered or not. Self managed participants are not bound.
- **GST** ([ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/in-detail/your-industry/gst-and-health/national-disability-insurance-scheme)): many supports are GST free, only when a plan is in effect, the support is in it, there is a written agreement and the item is in the Determination.
- **Claim time:** 2 years since 3 Oct 2024, **dropping to 90 days from 1 Dec 2026** ([ndis.gov.au](https://www.ndis.gov.au/ndis-laws/securing-ndis-future-generations)). How the 90 days is counted, and any transition, is not verified.

**Answer:** yes, in the browser. The fields are static and the checks are arithmetic. It needs the
2026-27 support catalogue (an XLSX of about 126 KB) as bundled JSON, refreshed when the NDIA
republishes it (at least yearly); a plan managed or self managed switch; GST free by default; a
date on each line; and a 90 day claim warning. It cannot check that a participant's number or
plan is valid.

## 5. Three twists, ranked

1. **Dated lines for people paid by the visit, with an NDIS layout.** Choose "NDIS" and the invoice
   gets participant name and number, a support item number and date on every line, GST free by
   default, the price limit checked, and a warning before the 90 day claim limit.
   *Why first:* the ndis searches return Word downloads and blog posts, not tools; the rules are
   fixed fields plus a catalogue file; the two NDIS apps found need an account. The same dated
   lines serve cleaners, tutors and gardeners on pages that already rank poorly in Australia.
   *Risks:* volume unknown; the catalogue must be refreshed each year; the rules must be read by
   hand first (ndis.gov.au blocked the agents); the 90 day rule starts 1 Dec 2026.
2. **Same again.** Open the page, tap a customer, and last time's invoice is ready with today's date
   and the next number. *Mostly built in Stage 0* ("New invoice for …" at the top). What is left:
   carry the last invoice's lines, not just the saved ones, and a list of recent invoices per
   customer with paid or unpaid. It is the reason to come back, not the reason to arrive: no one
   searches for it. Every rival with an account has saved customers; none without an account does.
3. **Type it like a text.** "3 hrs mowing for Sue at $60" becomes a filled invoice. No review or
   search asked for it, and it is the hardest to get exactly right. Keep it as a later delight,
   not the twist.

## 6. Recommended Pro list and price

**What rivals already give free with an account:** recurring invoices and reminders (Zoho AU,
Square AU), saved customers (almost all), sync (Zoho, Invoice Ninja). So Pro cannot charge much
for these. What users hate most is **price rises, caps and money held**, so the offer must be
cheap, uncapped and never touch the money.

**Pro, in build order:**
1. Customers, templates and invoices synced across phone and laptop, with paid or unpaid status.
2. Send from the product with a "viewed" receipt.
3. Recurring invoices sent on a schedule.
4. Automatic overdue reminders.

**Drop the pay now link.** "Money held" is the second biggest complaint, it ties the product to a
payment provider's rules (surcharges are banned from 1 Oct 2026, per Rounded's help page), and
bank details and PayID on the invoice already work for free.

**Price: AUD 5 a month or AUD 48 a year, no invoice caps, and the price never rises for anyone who
signs up early.** Reasoning: the cheapest plans that include viewed and reminders are Invoice2go
Starter (US$7.99 a month) and Invoice Home ($9, currency not stated); Rounded starts at $23.95.
Zoho and Square give similar features free, so Pro must be clearly cheaper than the paid rivals
and sell on what they lack: no account needed for the free part, no caps, no branding, no price
rises. Test AUD 5 in Stage 3 before building.
