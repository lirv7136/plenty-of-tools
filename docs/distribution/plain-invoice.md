## AlternativeTo listing

- **Name:** Plain Invoice
- **URL:** https://plaininvoice.com.au
- **Tagline:** Free invoice maker for Australian businesses. No account, nothing uploaded.
- **Alternative to:** Invoice Simple, Invoice2go, Zoho Invoice, Wave, Square Invoices, Rounded
- **Platforms:** Web, installable on phones (works offline)
- **Licence:** Free, open source (MIT)
- **Tags:** invoicing, invoice generator, quotes, GST, NDIS, small business, privacy
- **Description:**
  Plain Invoice makes Australian invoices and quotes in the browser. Add your ABN, choose GST or
  no GST, put BSB, account and PayID under How to pay, and download a real PDF or email it from
  your own mail app. Each line can carry its date, for work paid by the visit, and there is an
  NDIS layout with the participant's NDIS number and a support item number on every line. Save
  a customer once and the next invoice is one tap, with the new date and the next number. Your
  invoices are listed as paid or unpaid. Nothing is uploaded: everything stays in your browser,
  there is no account, and there is no limit on invoices.

## Post 1: for NDIS support worker groups (Facebook groups, r/NDIS if its rules allow)

**Title:** I made a free NDIS invoice maker for independent support workers

I kept seeing support workers build invoices in Word. I made a free tool that does the NDIS
parts for you: the participant's name and NDIS number, the date and support item number on
every line, your ABN, and plan managed or self managed. It will not make the PDF until every
line has its date and item number, and it warns you when a support is more than 90 days old.

It runs in your browser. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. It does not check price
limits, so you still need the current pricing arrangements for your items.

https://plaininvoice.com.au/ndis-invoice-template/

I would like to know what it gets wrong. What does your plan manager ask for that is missing?

## Post 2: for Australian small business communities (r/AusSmallBusiness or similar, if allowed)

**Title:** Free invoice maker with no account and no invoice cap (ABN, GST or no GST, PayID)

A family member needed to send a few invoices a month and every app either capped the free
plan or wanted a subscription. So I built one that runs entirely in the browser: ABN, GST or
no GST, BSB and PayID on the invoice, a date on each line for cleaners and tutors, and a real
PDF. Save a customer and next month's invoice is one tap.

Nothing is uploaded and there is no sign up. It is open source.

https://plaininvoice.com.au

Honest question for people who invoice every week: what would make you switch from what you
use now, and what would you never pay for?
