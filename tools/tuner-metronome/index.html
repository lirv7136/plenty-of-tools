Notes

  • Chromatic shows the nearest note of any pitch. An instrument preset instead shows the nearest string and how far you are from it, and highlights that string. Tap a string name to hear its reference tone.
  • Cents are hundredths of a semitone. Within about 3 cents counts as in tune here; most people cannot hear less than 5.
  • Reference pitch. Orchestras and older recordings sometimes use 442 or 415 Hz instead of 440. Change it and every note shifts with it.
  • Low strings (bass, drop tunings) take a moment longer to read because the tuner needs a few full cycles of the wave. Let the note ring.
  • Works offline. After your first visit this page is stored in your browser, so it loads and tunes with no signal at all. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
  • Settings are remembered on this device. Nothing else is stored.
