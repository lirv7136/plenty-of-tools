    How it works

    • Straightened, not just cropped. The four corners define the page; the tool undoes the camera’s angle so the page comes out square.
    • Document mode evens out shadows and uneven light and turns the page into clean black and white. Use Colour for photos and stamps.
    • Private by design. Photos are read in this tab and never sent anywhere. Pages are kept only until you close the tab.
    • The PDF is A4, one page per photo, at about 200 dpi.
