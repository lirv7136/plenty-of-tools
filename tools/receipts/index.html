How it works

  • Read on your device. The text reader (Tesseract, open source) runs in your browser. Photos never leave it, and they aren't kept after reading.
  • Check every row. Faded thermal paper and creased receipts trip any reader. The table is yours to edit; the CSV is what you see.
  • GST in grey is an estimate (one eleventh of the total) for receipts that don't state it. Only claim GST a tax invoice shows.
  • ABNs are checked with the ATO's checksum, so a misread number is left blank rather than wrong.
  • This device keeps an autosaved copy of the table. Clear removes it.
