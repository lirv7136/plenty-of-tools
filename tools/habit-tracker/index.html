How this counts

  • A rest day is not a miss. For a habit on chosen days, the days in between are skipped, not failed, so a Monday and Thursday run keeps its streak over the weekend.
  • Today stays open. A habit due today that you have not done yet still shows yesterday's streak. It only breaks once the day is over.
  • A weekly target counts in weeks. Three times a week means the streak is the number of consecutive weeks you hit three, and the week in progress counts as soon as you get there. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
  • Everything stays here. Habits and ticks live in this browser's storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account, so moving to another device means exporting the file and importing it there.
  • Works offline. After your first visit the page is stored in your browser, so it opens and ticks with no connection. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.
