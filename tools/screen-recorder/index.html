How this stays free and unlimited

  • Nothing is uploaded. The recording is written straight to a file on your computer as you record (in Chrome and Edge), or kept in the tab until you download it (Firefox and Safari).
  • No time cap, because there is no server storing your video. The only limit is your own disk.
  • Camera bubble and microphone are mixed in on your machine. You choose which devices, and the browser asks permission each time.
  • Sharing the result is up to you: Google Drive, YouTube unlisted, Slack, email. The paid tools' hosted link is convenient; it is also the reason they can put a timer on you.
  • Phones and tablets cannot record their screen from a browser, so this needs a laptop or desktop.
