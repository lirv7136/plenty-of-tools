About these readings

  • Measured, not forecast. These are live readings from ARPANSA’s UV detectors in each city. Cloud can change them minute to minute.
  • 3 or above means sun protection. Cancer Council Australia and ARPANSA recommend a hat, SPF 50+ sunscreen, sunglasses, shade and covering clothing whenever the UV index is 3 or higher.
  • The page refreshes every five minutes while it’s open. Your city is remembered on this device only.
