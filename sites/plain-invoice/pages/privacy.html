Privacy

Nothing you type into the invoice maker is uploaded. It runs in your browser.

What stays on your device

  • The invoice you are working on stays in the open tab. Closing or refreshing the tab clears it unless you save a draft file.
  • Saved customers, templates, your invoice list and your last invoice number are kept in this browser's storage, and only after you choose to save something. Delete them at any time with Delete everything saved here, at the bottom of the form.
  • PDFs and draft files you download go to your own device.

Email

When you email an invoice, the message opens in your own mail app, or your device's share sheet, or Gmail or Outlook in a new tab. It is sent by that app, not by this site.

What this site does collect

{{analytics_note}}

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which, like any web host, handles each request (your IP address and browser details) to deliver the page. This site does not set cookies, does not use advertising trackers and does not sell anything about you.
