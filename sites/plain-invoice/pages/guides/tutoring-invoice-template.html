Tutoring invoice template, one line per lesson

For tutors and music, swim or sport coaches in Australia: list each lesson with its date, send one invoice a week, a term or a month.

Make a tutoring invoice

How it works

  1. Put in your details and the parent or student once.
  2. Add each lesson as a line with its date. Another visit like the last copies the lesson, so you only pick the date.
  3. Download the PDF or email it, and save the details: next time it is one tap, with the new date and the next invoice number.
Example: weekly maths tutoring, not registered for GST.
  • 3 Nov · Year 9 maths, 1 hour at $60: $60.00
  • 10 Nov · Year 9 maths, 1 hour at $60: $60.00
  • 17 Nov · Year 9 maths, 1 hour at $60: $60.00
  • Total $180.00

Your invoices are listed with paid or unpaid, so you can see who still owes you.
