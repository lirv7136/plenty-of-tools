Cleaning invoice template, with the date of each clean

For home and office cleaners in Australia: one line per clean, each with its date, your ABN, and GST only if you are registered.

Make a cleaning invoice

How it works

  1. Your business details and the customer go in once.
  2. Each clean is a line with its date. Another visit like the last copies the line, so you only pick the new date.
  3. Download the PDF or email it. Tap Save these details for next time and next month the invoice is one tap away, with the new date and the next number.
Example: a fortnightly home clean, not registered for GST.
  • 4 Nov · Home clean, 3 hours at $50: $150.00
  • 18 Nov · Home clean, 3 hours at $50: $150.00
  • Total $300.00, due in 7 days, paid by bank transfer
