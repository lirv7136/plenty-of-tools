Can you invoice without an ABN?

You can send an invoice without an ABN, but a business that pays you may have to hold back 47% of it. Here is what that means and what to do.

Make an invoice

What happens without an ABN

A business that pays you more than $75 (excluding GST) without your ABN on the invoice generally has to withhold 47% of the payment and send it to the ATO, unless an exception applies.

If you do not need an ABN

Some people are exempt from quoting one, for example when the work is a hobby. In that case you give the payer the ATO's statement by a supplier form with your invoice, so they do not have to withhold. The NDIS says the same for providers who are exempt: include that form with the invoice.

If you are in business

Put your ABN on every invoice. The invoice maker checks the number's format as you type and puts it under your business name.
