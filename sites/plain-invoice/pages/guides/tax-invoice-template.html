Tax invoice template for Australian businesses registered for GST

Registered for GST? Choose whether your prices include or exclude GST, and the invoice is headed Tax invoice with the GST worked out on each line.

Make a tax invoice

When you must give one

When you are registered for GST, you must give a tax invoice for a taxable sale over $82.50 including GST, or whenever the customer asks, within 28 days.

What it must show

  • the words "tax invoice"
  • your business name and ABN
  • the date it was issued
  • what you sold, with quantity and price
  • how much of each item is taxable
  • the GST payable, or a statement that the total includes GST
  • for sales of $1,000 or more, the buyer's identity or ABN

The invoice maker lays this out for you, rounds GST to the cent on each line, and lets you mark single lines as GST free.
